# Project Status — Emotion Concepts in Open-Weight LLMs

**Date:** 2026-06-15  ·  **Owner:** Haydar  ·  **Audience:** project team

Replication & extension of Sofroniew et al. (2026), *"Emotion Concepts and their
Function in a Large Language Model,"* on open-weight 7–8B models — testing whether
emotion concepts are (H1) linearly readable and (H2/H3/H7) causally steer behavior
("functional emotions" = behavior + representation, not subjective experience).

---

## TL;DR

The development-fleet gate is cleared and the concept-validation (C2) suite is built
and run on the dev models. The encouraging headline: the story-method emotion vectors
are **genuine concepts, not surface lexis** — they decode to emotion-congruent
vocabulary, fire in held-out contexts, and (for most emotions) track *semantic*
intensity against the raw number. The one open case is **admiration**. We are now at
the launch line for the **primary 7–9B run**, which is the decisive, reportable result
— and which needs two quick team decisions (model confirmation + budget sign-off).

## What we've established (dev fleet)

**Methods pivot — story method is primary.** A confound audit found the original CAA
short-vignette derivation surface-confounded (a length-only classifier reached AUC up
to 0.93; word/char TF-IDF 0.86–0.97). We switched to the paper-faithful **story
method** (topic-matched self-generated stories, emotion word banned, token-50
mean-pool, cross-emotion centering, neutral-PC projection), which neutralizes those
confounds by construction.

**Third primary fixed — Gemma 2 9B.** Chosen for dense + bf16 parity with Llama 3.1 8B
and Qwen 2.5 7B (clean cross-model geometry) and because it fits a single RTX 4090
without quantization. Gemma 3 4B (below the size bar, vision-language), 12B (won't fit
in bf16), and Gemma 4 E4B (Mixture-of-Experts → architectural confound) were rejected.

**Key finding — linear decodability is necessary but not sufficient.** Within the story
corpus, an emotion probe is *surface-saturated*: a bag-of-words classifier separates
emotion stories from neutral (TF-IDF 0.91–1.00), and the activation probe beats it by a
vanishing margin that **shrinks with model capability** (~0.04 on Qwen 0.5B → 0.00 on
Llama 1B). The pipeline itself is sound (shuffle-null AUC = 0.51). So a high probe AUC
alone does not establish an emotion *concept* — the claim has to be carried by
out-of-distribution validation (C2) and by causal steering.

**C2 validation results (dev).**

- **Logit-lens** (stimulus-free): vectors decode to emotion-congruent tokens on all
  three models (e.g. loathing → disgusting/hatred; sadness → grief/mourning), and the
  opposite-pair geometry (admiration↔loathing, joy↔sadness) emerges unsupervised.
  **Pass.**
- **Cross-context activation** (implicit scenarios at the Assistant-colon): 4-way argmax
  accuracy **0.53 → 0.68 → 0.72** (Qwen 0.5B / Llama 1B / Gemma 2B; chance 0.25), rising
  with capability. **Pass, scaling.**
- **Semantic intensity** (numerical-intensity templates, inverse families): the vector
  tracks *meaning against the digit* for **sadness (ρ ≈ +0.79, strongest), joy (+0.47),
  loathing (+0.26, weak)**, but **admiration is number-captured (−0.49)** at the
  conceptual layers — and a layer sweep shows that's robust, not a layer artifact.

**Per-emotion read (dev):**

| Emotion | Logit-lens | Cross-context | Semantic intensity | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Admiration | pass | mixed | number-captured | **open case** |
| Joy | pass | moderate | moderate | supported |
| Loathing | strong | strong | weak | supported |
| Sadness | pass | mixed | strong | strongest |

   number-captured pattern resolves at 7–9B** and whether the **number-magnitude
   confound shrinks with scale**.
3. **Behavioral steering (the causal test)** — H2 blackmail and H7 sycophancy↔harshness,
   dose-response at ≤ 0.1 × residual-stream norm, vs zero/random/orthogonal controls at
   n ≥ 200. This is what actually tests "functional."
4. **Later** — emotion-space geometry (valence/arousal), and broaden N by
   hand-authoring (not paraphrase) if more power is needed.
